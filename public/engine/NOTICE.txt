NEON AI chess uses Stockfish.js, a WebAssembly build of the Stockfish chess engine.

  Stockfish        https://stockfishchess.org/            (GNU General Public License v3 or later)
  Stockfish.js     https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js (GNU General Public License v3 or later)
  Engine version   Stockfish.js 10 (npm package "stockfish" 10.0.2), unmodified.

The full text of the GPL is in COPYING.txt in this folder. The source code for the engine is available at the addresses above.
The rest of NEON AI talks to the engine only through its standard text (UCI) interface.
